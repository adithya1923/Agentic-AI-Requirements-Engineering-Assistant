Branch and digital teams discussed how a customer transfer should be reviewed.

One stakeholder said: “Payments above a large amount should be checked quickly by another authorized employee. For smaller payments, the system should use the appropriate review.” No one explained what “large,” “quickly,” or “appropriate” means.

Another stakeholder said: “Every payment must always have a second employee approval before it goes out.” The first stakeholder replied that routine low-value transfers should not be held up. The group did not decide which rule to use or whether the rules apply to the same payment channels.

The product owner also asked for a simple way to tell customers when a payment is complete. The service team asked what should happen when the external payment provider does not respond. The meeting ended before either question was resolved.
